303BOX / LEARN BY DOING

Build a pattern. Change one step. Hear the result.

Finish this workshop with two versions: a nine-note A minor phrase and an eight-note variation with a rest on step six. Compare them at the same tempo, then write down which you prefer and why. No hardware is required.

Written by Z3ZFirst published 27 August 2026Updated 23 October 2026

1. Set up a fair comparison

Choose browser playback; this exercise needs no MIDI permission. Set tempo to 120 BPM and Tune to 0. Keep waveform and sound controls fixed throughout the comparison. Setting Delay, Reverb and Distortion to zero makes the rests easier to hear. These are listening choices, not a universal acid patch.

The loading links ask before replacing your bass pattern and provide Undo for the last example load. They preserve tempo, rhythm and sound controls. Save an important sketch separately with DOWNLOAD before experimenting.

3. Change only step six

Listen to A for a few loops. Then remove the E on step 6 and make its gate a rest. The higher A on step 8 now arrives after a longer gap. The E on step 11 stays in place, letting you compare how the same pitch serves the second half of the phrase.

This change does not prescribe a winner. A answers more frequently in the first half; B leaves more space before step 8. Decide by the feel of that entrance, not simply by counting notes.

4. Compare the recording with your grid

  1. Play recording A, then B. Each contains two bars at 120 BPM.
  2. Open either loading link and confirm. Press PLAY in the 303 section; stop the rhythm part for a bass-only comparison.
  3. Watch step 6: B should be silent there and A should sound E. The octaves of steps 8 and 15 stay the same.
  4. If the tone differs from the recording, compare note order and rests first. The clips use a separate educational software synth; they do not reproduce your current browser patch or a TD-3.

5. Keep a decision, then choose the next experiment

Name the result something useful, such as “A — denser opening” or “B — space before step 8.” In EFX / Notes, write the single edit and what you heard. DOWNLOAD saves a pattern image, not an audio recording or a device backup.

Check your work: is the final step a rest in both versions? Did only step 6 change? Did tempo or filter movement affect your judgment? Once those checks hold, try the accent and slide comparisons with notes held constant. To place the bass against drums, continue with the sixteen-bar arrangement exercise.
