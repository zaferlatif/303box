303 workflow

A strong acid pattern starts with rhythm, not complexity.

The most convincing lines use a small pitch vocabulary and let timing, accent, slide and filter movement create the character.

01

Create space

Use rests before adding more notes. A good 303 line needs negative space to bounce.

02

Choose the pressure points

Accent only the steps that should punch through. Slide should connect intentional pitch targets.

03

Perform the filter

Keep resonance alive and move cutoff, envelope modulation and decay while the loop repeats.

FAQ

Built to get out of the way.

303box is deliberately small: no account, no project setup and no software installation.

What is 303box?

A free browser-based 16-step acid bassline pattern generator inspired by classic TB-303 and TD-3 workflows.

Does it generate audio?

Yes. The preview engine uses the Web Audio API so you can audition a pattern before taking it to hardware.

Can I export my pattern?

Yes. Download the complete pattern sheet as a JPG including notes, knob positions and session notes.

Where is my pattern stored?

Your current session is stored locally in your browser so a refresh does not immediately erase your work.
