WHAT 303BOX IS

A notebook with sound — not a replacement for the musician.

The random buttons use simple musical rules to create a starting point; they are not an AI composition model. 303box exists to make tiny-step editing easier, let you hear the sketch, and help you move that idea onto hardware. The musical decisions and the performance remain yours.

01Sketch faster

Type and edit a 16-step idea on a large screen instead of entering every step through a small hardware interface.

02Preview, do not outsource

Hear the pattern, change it, reject it, rebuild it. The preview is a workspace for decisions, not a finished performance.

03Finish on hardware

Send or record the pattern into supported gear, then shape, perform and save it on the device itself.

Operational disclaimerKullanım sorumluluğu

303box is a sketching and preview tool. MIDI, SysEx, backup/restore and other hardware workflows can change device state or overwrite a target pattern. Back up important data, verify the selected device and slot, and test on non-critical patterns first. Browser audio/timing and third-party hardware behavior can vary between systems.

303box bir pattern taslak ve önizleme aracıdır. MIDI, SysEx, yedekleme/geri yükleme ve diğer donanım akışları cihaz durumunu değiştirebilir veya hedef pattern’in üzerine yazabilir. Önemli verileri yedekleyin, seçili cihazı ve slotu doğrulayın, ilk denemeleri önemsiz pattern’lerde yapın. Tarayıcı ses/zamanlama davranışı ve üçüncü taraf donanımlar sistemler arasında farklılık gösterebilir.
