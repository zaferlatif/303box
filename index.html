303 BASICS

A focused 303 workflow

A convincing acid line is usually controlled repetition, rhythmic gaps and a few deliberate moments of movement.

1. Build the rhythm first

Start with a small note set and use rests to create space. Make the rhythm work before filter movement.

2. Add accent and slide

Accent selected hits for impact and use slide sparingly to connect pitch targets.

3. Perform the filter

Keep resonance present, then move cutoff and envelope modulation until the loop feels animated.

Classic acid sequencing is less about complex harmony and more about how gate length, rests, octave changes, accent and slide push one step into the next.
